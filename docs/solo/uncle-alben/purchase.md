## The Level 13 Shopping Plan

**Budget:** about **3,700 gp** (the level-13 export, `…QX-3.json`, records 3,706 gp plus change on hand). This replaces the old 5,000 gp plan, part of which he has already spent on other things. Update [inventory.md](inventory.md#currency) from the next export, not by hand.

**Level cap:** items up to **level 14** *(set when he was level 12; at 13th the same two-above rule would allow level 15)*. Level 13–14 items are marked **L13** / **L14**. Several are now within his own level.

**Ground rules this plan respects:**
- **ABP is in play.** No potency, striking, or resilient runes; they don't exist in this campaign. Property runes do, and they're the only way to spend gold on weapons and armor.
- **Property rune slots come from ABP potency.** Under ABP an item holds as many property runes as the matching potency bonus: **Attack Potency +2 → 2 weapon runes, Defense Potency +2 → 2 armor runes.** The maul has 1 free slot (flaming takes the other) and the Full Plate has 2 free. *Confirm with the GM; this is the rule as written for ABP, but tables vary.*
- **Hands are the real constraint.** Maul = both hands. The Holy Prayer Beads need a hand. Wands, staves, and scrolls need a hand. Anything activated as a reaction must **already be in hand**, since drawing it is an Interact action he can't fit inside a reaction.
- **Skill Potency (ABP) is an item bonus**, so skill-boosting items overlap with it. Those are in "Skip" below.

### Step 0 — Free: decide what to prepare

- **Breath of Life** is **no longer on his sheet** (see [spells.md](spells.md#known-spells-as-recorded-in-the-export)). If the player still wants the prepared reaction revive, re-add the spell to Foundry first. A prepared reaction beats a scroll or wand of it, which would have to be in hand when someone drops.
- **Truesight** is now on the sheet (6th rank) and the social loadout has an open 6th-rank slot for it, so the old Scroll of Truesight line is dropped from this plan.
- **Fly** and **Unfettered Movement** are known but not prepared at 13th. A prepared copy of either is free, so check the combat list before buying a wand of Fly.

## Skip — and why

| Item | Why not |
|------|---------|
| Potency / striking / resilient runes | **They don't exist under ABP.** He already has their equivalents for free. |
| Armbands of Athleticism, Diplomat's Badge, and other +skill items | Item bonuses **overlap ABP Skill Potency** instead of stacking. Assign his four unassigned Skill Potency picks first (see [character.md](character.md#automatic-bonus-progression)), then buy only for skills potency isn't covering. |
| Replacing the Wooden Shield | The shield is gone at 13th, and no hand is free for one with the maul or the rapier-and-beads grip. Shield Block stays inert unless the player decides otherwise. |
| Shifting rune (to turn the maul into a rapier) | Shifting only changes a weapon into one needing the **same number of hands**. Maul is two-handed and the rapier is one-handed. Buy the 2 gp rapier instead. |
| Keen rune **L13** | Within the level cap, but the maul can't take it (keen needs piercing or slashing, and the maul is bludgeoning). It *is* legal on the rapier, but 3,000 gp to widen the crit range on his backup weapon is the wrong priority. |
| Scroll or wand of Breath of Life | It's a reaction, so it only works if the item is already in hand when someone drops. Preparing the spell (Step 0) is free and always works, once it's back on the sheet. |
| Scroll of Truesight (Rank 6) | Truesight is on the sheet now. |
| Wand of Haste, Invisibility, Tailwind, Sleeves of Storage | Already bought (see below). |
| Potion of Disguise (Greater) | Dropped from the list earlier; still not needed. Lesser and Moderate cover his escape plan. |
