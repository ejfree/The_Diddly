## The 5,000 gp Shopping Plan

**Budget:** 5,000 gp. *(The latest export, `…QX-2.json`, records about 771 gp on hand, so this budget assumes new funds that aren't on the sheet yet. Update [inventory.md](inventory.md#currency) from the next export, not by hand.)*

**Level cap:** items up to **level 14** (two above his current level 12). Level 13–14 items are marked **L13** / **L14** so they're easy to spot if availability tightens.

**Ground rules this plan respects:**
- **ABP is in play.** No potency, striking, or resilient runes; they don't exist in this campaign. Property runes do, and they're the only way to spend gold on weapons and armor.
- **Property rune slots come from ABP potency.** Under ABP an item holds as many property runes as the matching potency bonus: **Attack Potency +2 → 2 weapon runes, Defense Potency +2 → 2 armor runes.** The maul has 1 free slot (flaming takes the other) and the Full Plate has 2 free. *Confirm with the GM; this is the rule as written for ABP, but tables vary.*
- **Hands are the real constraint.** Maul = both hands. The Holy Prayer Beads need a hand. Wands, staves, and scrolls need a hand. Anything activated as a reaction must **already be in hand**, since drawing it is an Interact action he can't fit inside a reaction.
- **Skill Potency (ABP) is an item bonus**, so skill-boosting items overlap with it. Those are in "Skip" below.

### Step 0 — Free: prepare Breath of Life

Before spending anything: **Breath of Life is already on his known-spell list** (see [spells.md](spells.md#known-spells-as-recorded-in-the-export)) and prepared in neither loadout. Swap it into a 5th-rank Combat slot (displacing the 5th-rank Harm or Marvelous Mount, since he now owns a Marvelous Mount wand). A prepared reaction revive beats any scroll or wand of it, because those would have to be held in hand when someone drops.

## Skip — and why

| Item | Why not |
|------|---------|
| Potency / striking / resilient runes | **They don't exist under ABP.** He already has their equivalents for free. |
| Armbands of Athleticism, Diplomat's Badge, and other +skill items | Item bonuses **overlap ABP Skill Potency** instead of stacking. Assign his three unassigned Skill Potency picks first (see [character.md](character.md#automatic-bonus-progression)), then buy only for skills potency isn't covering. |
| Any shield upgrade | Unusable alongside the maul, and the rapier/beads grip has no hand left for it either. |
| Shifting rune (to turn the maul into a rapier) | Shifting only changes a weapon into one needing the **same number of hands**. Maul is two-handed and the rapier is one-handed. Buy the 2 gp rapier instead. |
| Keen rune **L13** | Within the level cap, but the maul can't take it (keen needs piercing or slashing, and the maul is bludgeoning). It *is* legal on the rapier, but 3,000 gp to widen the crit range on his backup weapon is the wrong priority. |
| Scroll or wand of Breath of Life | It's a reaction, so it only works if the item is already in hand when someone drops. Preparing the spell (Step 0) is free and always works. |
| Potion of Disguise (Greater) | Dropped from the list earlier; still not needed. Lesser and Moderate cover his escape plan. |
