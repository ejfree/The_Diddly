A mountain of a man in full plate, a wine-cask holy symbol swinging from his belt, who blesses his allies with a swig of his own rambutan brandy — "Rambui" to everyone who's ever shared a cup with him — before a fight, and beats the mercy of Cayden Cailean into anyone who threatens his flock. Loud, warm, generous to a fault, and slightly drunk at all times — but never too drunk to smash a skull with a maul or knit a friend's ribs back together. Everyone in the party just calls him Uncle Alben.

Why an orc worships an outsider god: Orc worship of their own pantheon is actually uncommon in Golarion lore — most orc deities are grim, war-obsessed Belkzen gods whom orcs see as just another target worth challenging rather than reverent figures. Less violent orc holds often adopt outside gods instead (Sarenrae is the classic example). Alben is cut from that same cloth: he took one look at the dour, lightning-throwing, grudge-keeping Belkzen pantheon and decided he'd rather follow a god who throws a party. Cayden Cailean's creed of freedom, courage, and generosity suited him a lot better than a childhood spent being told to hate everything with a face.

His pet is solomon. A mandarin duck w/a collar of empathy. Usually rides on a special nest/perch on alben's shoulder.
