# Planar Palace Expansions

> **Planning Document — Future Idea.** This is not built and Alben doesn't have the wand. It's an idea for later expansions of the [First Church](../firstchurch.md), kept here so it doesn't muddy the real building. Where it disagrees with `firstchurch.md`, that file wins.

Roll **1d20** at the opening ceremony each day to choose the day's arena. In actuality, Uncle Alben spins a wheel beside the portal and whatever it lands on is the choice for the day. The [Foundry macro](../../assets/scripts/arena-roll.js) does it and posts the result to chat.

## Tuning the Arenas

Every arena has a **circumstance bonus** (**+B**) and a **hazard**, and both scale together with the **arena level**. **Alben sets the level**, in the morning before the roll, to match who is fighting that day. Pick one of five settings:

| Arena level | Bonus (+B) | Hazard DC | Hazard damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | +1 | 13 | 1d6 |
| **L3** | +1 | 16 | 2d6 |
| **L5** | +2 | 18 | 3d6 |
| **L7** | +2 | 21 | 4d6 |
| **L9** | +3 | 24 | 5d6 |

## The Opening Ceremony

The casting is a **formal ceremony every day**, and it is what officially **opens the church** for the new day. Nothing is bet, bought, or fought until it's done.

- **The portal** appears on the **north wall of the public room, in the center**.
- **The fight cards** hang on **boards flanking it**, one on the **left** and one on the **right**. The cards list the day's bouts, with the arena named at the top, once it's rolled.
- **The sequence.** Alben lights the front lanterns and rakes the Pit. He **spins the wheel** in front of whoever is there. He **imagines the rolled arena** and **casts the wand** (one minute), and the shimmering doorway rises in the center of the wall. The cards go up on the boards, and he pours a drink at the **altar** and puts the offering bucket out.
- **After the casting,** the **church is open.** The doorway stays up until the next daily preparations.
- **The designs.** Alben has **twenty arenas rehearsed**, so the roll only decides which one he imagines as he casts. See [What the Spell Gives Him](#what-the-spell-gives-him).

## The Idea

The Pit is fifteen feet across. A **wand of *Planar Palace*** gives the church a second venue every day, because the spell lets the caster **design the palace's floor plan** as they cast it. Each day's roll picks the arena, the shimmering doorway goes up, and some portion of that night's card is held there. Some wait for a special day to fight, Alben loves the randomness of it all. 

### The Arena Space

Every arena is built in the same space, **48 feet wide at the rear wall, 50 feet along each side, and 20 feet tall**. The **door is in the entrance wall, opposite the rear wall**. Guests sit on the **two wings and the far side**, so the fight has the whole middle and nobody has to look past the door.

- **Volume:** 48 × 50 × 20 is **48,000 cubic feet**, the same as the spell's published **40 × 40 × 30** box. The floor is bigger (2,400 sq ft against 1,600) and the ceiling is lower. See the GM note below.
- **Guest strip:** **10 feet** all the way around the **two sides and the rear wall**, which gives guests a view from three sides.
- **Combat floor:** what's left is **28 feet wide by 40 feet deep**, or **1,120 sq ft**, open on the door end.

| Area | Size | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| **Combat floor** | 28 × 40 ft | The arena itself |
| **Side terraces** (left and right) | 10 × 40 ft each | **Three levels of terraced standing**, each a little higher than the last, running from the rear corner to the entrance wall |
| **Rear tables** | 28 × 10 ft | **Three levels of small tables**, stepped up toward the rear wall, taking **the whole rear wall between the restrooms** |
| **Restrooms** (rear left and right corners) | 10 × 10 ft each | One in each **top corner**, at the ends of the rear wall |
| **Beverage stands** (entrance wall) | against the wall | One on **each side of the door** |
| **Total** | 48 × 50 ft | 2,400 sq ft |

```
              48 ft  (rear wall)
     +------+--------------+------+
     |  WC  |  small tables |  WC  |
     |      |  (3 levels)   |      |
     +------+--------------+------+
     | T    |              |    T |
     | E    |              |    E |
     | R    |  COMBAT FLOOR|    R |   50 ft
     | R    |   28 x 40 ft |    R |   (each side)
     | A    |              |    A |
     | C    |              |    C |
     | E    |              |    E |
     | S    |              |    S |
     +[bar]-+---- door ----+-[bar]+
```

- **Capacity.** My estimate is about **120 standing** on the side terraces (one person per two feet of each 40-foot level, six levels in all) and about **30 seated** at the rear tables (three rows of five small tables, two seats each). Change it as you like.
- **Restrooms.** One in each rear corner, entered from the top of the nearest terrace. They're dressed to match the arena, and nobody lingers in the Boneyard's.
- **Beverage stands.** One against the entrance wall on each side of the door, serving the terraces and the floor's edge. **They serve drink and nothing else**, since no food is served at the church. The servants carry cups out from them.
- **Theming.** The guest strip is the same layout in every arena, dressed to match it. The Boneyard's terraces hold the dead, the Winter Court's are ice, the Cloud Arena's are floating ledges, and so on.
- **Height.** At 20 feet the ceiling is lower than the old 30. Arenas that need more headroom, like the Cloud Arena and the Heartwood, **fill the space by looking taller**, not by being taller.

> **GM Note — the space.** The spell text I found said the mansion fits a **40 × 40 × 30** space ([Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonsrd.com/spells/221-planar-palace), unchecked against the book). **48 × 50 × 20** has the **same volume** but isn't inside that box, since two sides are longer than 40 feet. This is a **house ruling** that treats the limit as cubic footage, not as a bounding box. Check the spell text before relying on it.

### House Rules for Every Palace

- **Where the doorway goes.** It opens in the **center of the public room's north wall**, flanked by the left and right fight cards (see [The Opening Ceremony](#the-opening-ceremony)). A bout in the palace can still counts toward the [Ladder](../firstchurch.md#the-shrine) if that is part of the agreement.
- **Betting and buffs.** Everything in [Fighting & Gambling](../firstchurch.md#fighting--gambling) still applies. The venue is announced at the **opening ceremony**, so both fighters know it before they agree, and it's part of the **tale of the tape**.
- **The Promise holds.** *Nobody dies in my house. Nobody stays broken past sunup.* The palace is part of the house, and Alben heals in there like anywhere else.
- **The door opens from inside.** Solomon's first rule. Anyone can walk out of the doorway at any time, with no hand laid on them.
- **The servants pour, and nothing else.** The 24 servants may serve **drinks**. **No food is served at the church**, palace included.
- **Spectators.** Guests watch from the terraced wings and the rear tables (see [The Arena Space](#the-arena-space)), so the combat floor stays clear. Anyone in the public room can also watch through the doorway.

### 1. The Starstone Cathedral

**Inspiration:** the **Test of the Starstone** in the Cathedral in Absalom, which made Cayden Cailean a god after he stumbled in drunk on a dare.

A **vaulted stone nave** with a glowing **starstone** on a dais at the rear wall, set among the rear tables. The combat floor is a **ring of three concentric rings**, and a fighter who wins a ring may step up to the next. The center ring is the smallest and most dangerous. Fights are **first to the starstone**, not to a knockout. The side terraces are worn stone pews, and the rear tables are old cathedral benches.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to Climb or Leap up to the next ring.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the starstone pulses. A fighter in the center ring makes a Will save or takes hazard force damage and is dazzled until the end of its next turn.

### 2. The Drowned Colosseum

**Inspiration:** **Azlant**, the sunken empire, and the **Plane of Water**.

A **flooded arena basin**, glass-walled along both sides and the rear, so guests on the terraces and at the rear tables watch through the glass like an aquarium. The room is sealed under a dome of air, and the lower two-thirds is **water**. The end by the door is a dry ledge. The servants are **water-spirits** that carry drinks to the dry ledge and along the guest strip. Fighters pick **air or water** before the bout.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to Swim.
- **Hazard:** At the start of each round an undertow sweeps the flooded half. A fighter in the water makes a Reflex save or is dragged 10 feet and takes hazard bludgeoning damage from the glass wall.

### 3. The Brass Furnace

**Inspiration:** the **Plane of Fire** and the **City of Brass**, the efreeti city.

A **brass-plated forge-hall** with a **river of molten metal** running through the combat floor and **narrow bridges** across it. The air is hot enough to hurt, and the walls are hung with chains and bellows. The side terraces are brass steps behind a low heat-shield rail, and the rear tables sit on a brass platform. Servants are **fire-spirits** pouring tankards of glowing spirits. Fighters can't stay in long.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves against heat.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round slag sprays from the river. A fighter on a bridge or beside the river makes a Reflex save or takes hazard fire damage.

### 4. The Cloud Arena

**Inspiration:** the **Plane of Air** and the cloud giants' floating citadels.

A **chain of floating stone platforms** drifting in open sky, joined by narrow bridges, with **no walls**. The combat platforms fill the middle, and the guests sit on **floating ledges** along both sides and on a rear platform with small tables. The servants are **air-spirits**, and a steady wind blows from one side. Whoever falls off the edge **drops through the floor of the palace** and is caught on a net 10 feet down.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks to Balance.
- **Hazard:** At the start of each round a gust sweeps the platforms. A fighter makes a Reflex save or is pushed 10 feet and takes hazard bludgeoning damage. A fighter pushed off the edge falls to the net.

### 5. The Shadow Court

**Inspiration:** the **Shadow Plane**, the dim echo of the Material Plane.

A **dim ballroom** of dark stone and black glass, lit only by **pale-blue lanterns**, where **everyone's shadow** moves a half-beat late. The side terraces are tiers of black stone, and the rear tables sit in deep shadow. The servants are **shadow-servants** that never speak. Fighters fight in the half-dark.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Perception checks to tell a fighter from its shadow.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the shadows grasp. Each fighter makes a Will save or takes hazard void damage and is off-guard until the end of its next turn.

### 6. The Glade of the First World

**Inspiration:** the **First World**, the realm of the fey.

An **oak glade** under a **permanent twilight sky**, ringed by toadstools and hung with glowing lanterns, with a **mossy ring** in the middle of the combat floor instead of a pit. The side terraces are mossy banks and root steps, and the rear tables are tucked among the toadstools. The servants are **sprites** with tiny cups.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Will saves against fey effects and curses.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the briars around the mossy ring lash out. A fighter at the edge of the ring makes a Reflex save or takes hazard piercing damage and is immobilized until the end of its next turn.

### 7. The Infernal Ledger

**Inspiration:** **Hell**, **Nessus**, and the devils who rule by **contract**.

A **tiered chamber of black marble and red lacquer**, built like a **courtroom**. The side terraces are the **gallery benches**, and the rear tables are for counsel, with the **long ledger** on the judge's bench in the center of them. The servants are **imps** in clerks' robes.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Will saves against coercion and mental effects.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round hellfire gutters up through the floor grates. A fighter makes a Reflex save or takes hazard fire damage.

### 8. The Writhing Maw

**Inspiration:** the **Abyss** and the **demon lords**, where the ground itself is hungry.

A raw red cavern of wet stone and exposed ribs, with a **floor that shifts** and sinks without warning. Only the combat floor moves. The guest terraces and rear tables are carved ledges of bone and stone that stay put. The servants are **quasits** that dart between guests with cups. The combat floor reshapes itself between rounds.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to Climb out of a gap or Escape.
- **Hazard:** At the start of each round, the room moves. Each round the GM rolls a d6 for what shifts (a gap opens, a ridge rises, the floor tilts), so nobody fights from the same footing twice. After the d6 shift, a fighter standing in a newly opened gap makes a Reflex save or falls in and takes hazard acid damage. A fighter on a tilted floor makes a Reflex save or is knocked prone. 

### 9. The Boneyard Court

**Inspiration:** the **Boneyard**, **Pharasma's** realm, where the dead are judged.

A grey spire hall of pale stone. The living guests sit on the side terraces and at the rear tables, and **silent, translucent spectators** from the Boneyard fill every gap between them: the dead, watching. The servants are ghostly attendants who pour for the living and set a cup out for the dead. A fighter who is knocked out sees the court's long hall for a heartbeat.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Will saves against fear.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round a grave-chill rolls through the hall. A fighter makes a Fortitude save or takes hazard void damage and is enfeebled 1 until the end of its next turn.

### 10. The Glowcap Caverns

**Inspiration:** the **Darklands**, the vast underground beneath Golarion.

A natural cavern under a low vault of dripping stone, lit by **glowing mushrooms** in blue, green, and violet, with spore clouds hanging in the air and pools of cold water between the pillars on the combat floor. The side terraces are natural stone shelves, and the rear tables sit on a flat fungus-covered ledge. The servants are fungal attendants who offer cups of fermented spore-brew.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Stealth checks.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the spores burst. A fighter makes a Fortitude save or takes hazard poison damage and is sickened 1.

### 11. The Axis Tribunal

**Inspiration:** **Axis**, the plane of law, and its **inevitables** and **axiomites**.

A perfectly rectangular hall of **white marble** with a spotless floor, exact right angles, and a **mechanical referee** standing in the center of the combat floor. The side terraces are precise white steps, and the rear tables are set in a perfect grid. The servants are axiomites. Everything is measured, and nothing is random.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Perception and Recall Knowledge checks.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round a measured pulse of force rolls across the floor from the walls. A fighter makes a Fortitude save or takes hazard force damage and is slowed 1 until the end of its next turn.

### 12. The Winter Court

**Inspiration:** **Irrisen**, the land of eternal winter ruled by the **witches of Baba Yaga**.

A ballroom of **blue ice** with a frozen floor, glittering chandeliers, and a throne of frost against the rear wall, with the small tables stepped up in front of it. The side terraces are ice steps hung with furs. The servants are frost-faced courtiers who hold steaming cups and never melt. Anyone who falls on the ice keeps sliding.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to Shove or Reposition.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the floor ices over and the frost bites. A fighter makes a Reflex save or falls prone on the slick ice and takes hazard cold damage.

### 13. The Alkenstar Gunworks

**Inspiration:** **Alkenstar**, the city of gunsmiths and alchemists in the Mana Wastes.

A mechanical arena of pipes, gantries, and furnaces, with **catwalks above** the combat floor and a firing range below. The side terraces are scaffold bleachers, and the rear tables sit on a gantry. The servants are **clockwork porters** that carry cups on trays. Fireworks, cannon, and alchemical charges go off on a timer.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to ranged attack rolls.
- **Hazard:** There are bangs and flashes on a schedule. On the blast round of the three-round cycle, a fighter in the blast zone makes a Reflex save or takes hazard damage (fire & sonic) and is deafened until the end of its next turn.

### 14. The Monsoon Hold

**Inspiration:** Alben's own homeland, the jungle islands of **Minata** and **Atas Pulu**, and the Rainfall orcs (see [background.md](../../background.md)).

A timber hold on stilts in a jungle clearing, under a roof of huge leaves with **warm rain** falling through the canopy. The combat floor is slick wet timber **walkways** laid over a flooded clearing, with ropes, vines, and beams overhead. The side terraces are timber tiers on stilts, and the rear tables sit on a raised deck, all above the water. The servants are jungle orcs in woven cloaks, pouring from bamboo flasks.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks to Balance and Nature checks (Alben's own +2 Rainfall heritage bonus to Climb and Swim is the same type and doesn't stack).
- **Hazard:** A fighter who falls or is pushed off the walkways into the flooded clearing is set upon by piranha-like fish. At the start of each of its turns in the water it makes a Reflex save or takes hazard piercing damage.

### 15. The Dreaming Hall

**Inspiration:** the **Dimension of Dreams**, where thought shapes the world.

A hall of soft light and drifting cloth, with no fixed walls and a **ceiling of slow stars**. The side terraces are tiers of cushions, and the rear tables float a hand above the floor. The servants are dream-attendants with changing faces. No one is truly hurt here: wounds fade when a fighter wakes, and a knockout is a nap.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Reflex saves.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round a nightmare passes through. A fighter makes a Will save or takes hazard mental damage (nonlethal, like all damage here) and is frightened 1.

### 16. The Verdant Heartwood

**Inspiration:** the **Plane of Wood**, where the world is one living forest.

The hollow heart of a **giant tree**, with living walls of bark, floors of roots, and a canopy of glowing leaves overhead. The side terraces are tiers of root and burl, and the rear tables sit on a broad branch-shelf. The servants are dryad-like tenders with cups of sap-brewed liquor. Roots and vines move slowly across the combat floor.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves.
- **Hazard:** At the start of each round the vines lash out. A fighter that ended its last turn standing still makes a Reflex save or takes hazard piercing damage and is immobilized until the end of its next turn.

### 17. The Maelstrom Roulette

**Inspiration:** the **Maelstrom**, the plane of chaos, where nothing stays put.

A spinning, ever-changing hall where the **walls, floor, and ceiling of the combat floor swap places** every few rounds. The guest terraces and rear tables hold steady. The servants are protean-like attendants that change shape. The only constant is the bell.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round a chaos burst crackles. A fighter makes a Reflex save or takes hazard damage of a random type (d4: fire, cold, electricity, sonic).

### 18. The Silver Mount Hangar

**Inspiration:** **Numeria** and the **Silver Mount**, the crashed starship whose strange technology litters the land.

A vast **metal hangar** of humming panels and flickering lights, its walls scarred by an old crash. The combat floor is laid with **gravity plates** that lighten whatever stands on them. The side terraces are gantry bleachers, and the rear tables sit on a raised platform. The servants are **robotic attendants** that pour drinks from dispensers built into their chests. Nobody here is sure what half the machinery does.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to High Jump and Long Jump.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round a laser grid sweeps the hangar. A fighter makes a Reflex save or takes hazard fire damage.

### 19. The Forge of the Metal Plane

**Inspiration:** the **Plane of Metal**, where the world is iron, steel, and gleaming ore.

A vast forge-hall of gears, anvils, and chains, with **magnetic plates** in the combat floor and showers of sparks. The side terraces are stone steps behind iron rails, and the rear tables sit behind an iron screen. The guest strip isn't magnetized. The servants are clockwork smiths that ring a bell every round. Anyone in metal armor feels the pull.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Strikes made with a metal weapon.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the plates surge. A fighter in metal armor or holding a metal weapon makes a Reflex save or takes hazard bludgeoning damage and is pulled prone.

### 20. The Accidental Hall

**Inspiration:** **Elysium**, the home plane of **Cayden Cailean** (see [planar.md](../../plans/planar.md)).

A vast timber tavern hall with a fire that never burns down in a hearth at the rear wall and a **ring in the middle** of the combat floor. The side terraces are packed benches, and the rear tables are long, scarred, and crowded, with a hundred stools between them. The servants are happy, tipsy attendants who pour freely and don't judge. It's every church Alben has ever imagined, all at once.

- **Bonus:** +B circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks.
- **Hazard:** At the end of each round the crowd surges. A fighter makes a Fortitude save or takes hazard bludgeoning damage (nonlethal) and is pushed 5 feet.
