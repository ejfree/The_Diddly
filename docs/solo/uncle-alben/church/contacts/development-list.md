# Development Contact List

> Who Alben should talk to while he remodels the [First Church](../firstchurch.md) in Goka's red light district. **Only people already established in this folder have names.** Everyone else is a role, left blank on purpose so it can be named at the table. Each entry says why Alben needs them, what he should ask, and how he should open.

**How Alben opens every one of these:** he claps his hands and asks *"Let's have a drink. You want the usual?"* (see [the ritual](../../rambui/liquor.md#the-ritual-lets-have-a-drink)). He makes the offer first and asks for the favor second, and he does it the same way for a council member and a night-soil carter.

---

## 1. Named Contacts: The Goka Sponsors

These five are recorded in [qualifiers.md](../../qualifiers.md) as the **Goka sponsors** he met at the Qualifiers. They are the only named people on this list, and all of them already know who he is.

| Contact | Recorded as | Why Alben needs them | What to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Malachai Dow** (she) | Business leader and teacher of mystic arts | Knows how business is done in Goka, and teaches magic. | Who to see for permits, and whether she knows anyone who can look at the **cats** without trying to remove them. |
| **Lord Aldenar Unmar** (he) | Alchemy mogul and potion maker | A supplier with money and a distillery's worth of equipment and contacts. | Copper, glassware, and still fittings for the back room. Alchemical solvents for cleaning a building this old. |
| **Lady Xhai Zhia** (she) | Fine arts patron and collector | Fine arts means craftspeople, painters, and woodworkers. | A carver for the **C²** mark and the tankard shrine icon. Decor for the bar that isn't ugly. |
| **Lady Mei** (she) | Noble | Standing. A noble who has been seen drinking in a place makes the district take it seriously. | A word with the district's officials, and whether she'd be seen at the opening. |
| **General Casohido** (he) | Mercenary leader | Fighters for the Pit, and a view on who controls the street. | Fighters who want bouts, and an honest read on the local gangs. |

> **GM Note — spelling.** Lord Aldenar's surname is recorded as "unmar" in lowercase in the Qualifiers notes. It's written "Unmar" here. Confirm the spelling, and the pronouns for Lady Xhai Zhia, which the notes give as "her" only.

> **GM Note — leads from the same notes.** `qualifiers.md` also mentions **Magambia School** and **Speakers to the Wind**. Nothing says what either one is, so neither is on this list, but both are worth a conversation if the player knows more.

## 2. City and Local Authority

All of these are roles. None is named.

| Role | Why | What to ask | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **District council or ward representative** | The council decides what a business in this district is allowed to be. | Whether a bar with a pit, a shrine, and free beds needs its own license, and who objects. | **First** |
| **Licensing office clerk** | Handles the paperwork for taverns, entertainment, and lodging. | Which of the church's functions count as a tavern, a fighting venue, and a lodging house. | **First** |
| **Building and permits inspector** | Signs off on structural work, and notices when something was built without it. | Permits for opening walls, adding a pit, and moving the stair. | **First** |
| **City watch: district captain** | The watch in this district has an arrangement with the street. | Whether the Pit needs a license, and what "left alone" costs. | High |
| **Fire warden or brigade** | A building full of spirits, casks, and a still is a fire risk. | Inspection, water access, and what the code requires for a still. | High |
| **Tax or levy collector** | Every business in the district is counted. | The levy for a bar and for a lodging house, and whether Quiet Rooms count. | Medium |
| **Notary or lawyer** | The deed and any claim on the building need to be clean. | Confirming title, the previous owner's debts, and whether the cats come with a claim. | **First** |

## 3. The Neighbors and the Street

| Role | Why | What to ask | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The operators of the houses on both sides** | Shared walls. They have been here longer than he has, and they are watching. | Noise hours, where deliveries go, and how they would like to be introduced to the church. | **First** |
| **The previous owner, or their heirs** | The building's history, and why it was cheap. | Who lived here, how they died, and why there are **cats**. | **First** |
| **Street-side shopkeepers** (food, laundry, tea) | The first customers and the first rumors. | Who delivers, who's honest, and who talks. | Medium |
| **Local healers or midwives** | They already treat the street's injuries. | Whether they'd refer patients to the Quiet Rooms, and what the street gets sick with. | Medium |
| **A trusted worker from one of the nearby houses** | The only way to learn who actually holds the debts. | Nothing, at first. Pour a drink and listen. | Medium |

## 4. The Underworld

None of these are named. A district like this has them, and Alben's rules (**no hand laid on anyone leaving**, **every door opens from the inside**) make him a problem for the ones who profit from locked doors.

| Role | Why | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Whoever runs protection on the street** | Every business pays someone. Alben has not decided whether he will. | Meet them early, on his own ground, with a drink in hand. Don't promise payment and don't promise defiance. |
| **The debt-holders** | They own the people who work the street. The Quiet Rooms and the "no hand on anyone leaving" rule cut directly into them. | Learn who they are before the first walk-out arrives. |
| **Fences and smugglers** | Casks, copper, and fittings that don't need to be traced. Also a source of rumors. | Useful, but each one is a favor owed. |
| **The street's gang or gangs, if more than one** | The district is probably divided. Neighbors aren't all on the same side. | Ask General Casohido first. |
| **Fighters' handlers and bookmakers** | They run the bouts in the district already and will see the Pit as competition or an opportunity. | Offer a partnership on the Pit's terms: **nobody dies in my house, nobody stays broken past sunup.** |

| **Baker and general food supplier** | Something to eat at the bar. | Medium |

## 6. Faith, Spirits, and Healing

| Role | Why | What to ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Other shrines or temples of Cayden Cailean in Goka, if any** | Alben's church is a church. Another one may see him as a colleague or as a rival. | Whether they'd co-sign the church as a real shrine, and what the local rules are. |
| **Other faiths in the district** | Neighbors are neighbors. | Common courtesy, and whether any of them already shelter people on this street. |
| **A spirit medium or exorcist** | The cats. | **Not to remove them.** Alben wants to know what they are and what they want. If anyone proposes to banish them, he walks out. |
| **An undertaker** | Because someone died in this house. | Records of who, and when. |
| **Apothecary and alchemist (beyond Lord Aldenar)** | Bandages, salves, and the ordinary medicines the Quiet Rooms will need. | A standing supply account. |

## Suggested Order

1. **Before the first wall comes down:** the notary, the previous owner, the permit office, the district council, the neighbors, and whoever runs protection.
2. **During the build:** carpenter, mason, roofer, coppersmith, and the courier service, with the five sponsors for materials and introductions.
3. **Before opening:** cooper, bamboo supplier, rambutan importer, spirits wholesaler, baker, fight promoters, and one honest word with the watch captain.
4. **Ongoing:** healers, the spirit medium, and every worker who walks in at three in the morning.
