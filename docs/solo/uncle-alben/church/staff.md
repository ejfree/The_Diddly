## The Clerical Structure — C Cubed

Alben calls his god **C²** (C squared): Alcohol² + Battle² = **Cayden Cailean**. The staff are that god's clergy, so the staff are **C³**: **Cayden Cailean Clergy**. The god is the square and the clergy are the cube. The whole payroll is organized as a clergy with two sacraments, the **Cup** and the **Fist**, and a third body, the **Hearth**, that keeps the doors open for both. Every title below is a planning label. The existing job names (Warden, Keeper, Pit Boss) stay the same and each gets a clerical rank.

### The Two Sacraments and the Hearth

| Order | Sacrament | Departments |
|---|---|---|
| **Order of the Cup** | Hard liquor | Long Bar & floor service, house kitchen, distillery & casking, Museum / Factory Tour / Store |
| **Order of the Fist** | Battle | Pit operations & changeover crew, Training Hall & matchmaking, Door Wardens, Pit Bosses |
| **The Hearth** | Hospitality and the house itself | Casino dealers, the Cage, Bunks front desk, housekeeping & laundry, Operations office |

The **Keepers of the Promise** belong to all three. They are the church's healers, and the Promise is its one vow.

### Ranks

| Rank | Who | Role in the church |
|---|---|---|
| **The Host** | Alben | The head of C³ and the only ordained cleric of the house. He keeps the first twelve feet of the Long Bar, performs the Ruining alone, and reads the Sunup Ledger. |
| **The House** | The First Aspirant | Runs the whole church. She is the closest thing to a prioress: she assigns posts, tests Aspirants in the Octagon, and holds the roster. |
| **Wardens** (3) | Shift Wardens | One per Pour. Each leads their Pour's congregation like a presiding vicar. |
| **Keepers** (9) | Keepers of the Promise | Healing clergy, three per Pour. Clerics of Cayden Cailean or trained medics. |
| **Marshals** (3) | Pit Bosses | One per Pour. They run the Cask House and keep order at the tables. |
| **Aspirants** | Trainees (~12) | Novices working toward Warden, Keeper, or Vault trust. |
| **Brothers & Sisters of the House** | Everyone else on payroll | Staff of C³ in the three Orders. Few are ordained and none take vows beyond the house rules. |
| **Pilgrims** | Guests, fighters, regulars | The congregation. |
| **Licensed Arbiters** | Independent casters | Contractors, not C³. They sell spells in the pit and pay the house 25%. |

### Ritual Calendar

The three **Pours** are the church's services. The **Sunup Toast** is the daily communion. The **Nightly Promise** is the creed. The **drink-offering ritual** at every bar station is the liturgy of the Cup. Bouts under the pit's rules are the liturgy of the Fist.

## The Aspirant Requirement

An **Aspirant** is a novice of the church: anyone who comes to learn the whole operation instead of working a single post. [The First Aspirant](first-aspirant.md) was the first. At Megachurch scale the Aspirant is a staffing requirement, because three roles can only be filled from the Aspirant pipeline:

| Role | Count | Why it must be an Aspirant |
|---|---|---|
| The First Aspirant, *the House* | 1 | Runs Operations for all four structures. |
| Shift Wardens | 3 | One per Pour. Each is an Aspirant of long standing. |
| Keepers of the Promise | 9 | Three per Pour. Every Keeper was an Aspirant first. They are house staff, never contractors. |
| **Minimum standing Aspirant-trained staff** | **13** | |

To keep those 13 posts filled, the church also needs Aspirants in training. Neither source file gives a number, so the figures below are assumptions to tune:

- **Trainees in the pipeline:** about one trainee per filled post at any time, so roughly 12 Aspirants at various stages. They draw trained-rate wages (5 sp/day, ≈ 6 gp/day total) from the Training Hall and Distillery lines.
- **Vault-trusted:** only Aspirants who have gone rounds with the House in the Octagon are trusted with the Vault. Nobody, the House included, performs the Ruining. Alben does that alone.
- **Succession:** if a Warden or Keeper leaves, the replacement comes from the trainee pool, not from outside hiring.

## Planning Notes

- **The Promise is the largest cost.** The 9 Keepers cost 135 of 278 gp/day, nearly half the payroll, and they can't be contracted out. Losing Aspirants hurts the Promise before anything else.
- **The House is a single point of failure.** She holds the only roster and the only drawing of Solomon's Road, and she reads the Sunup Ledger when Alben is away.
- **Alben knows fewer names as headcount grows.** He knew every name at the Big Church and doesn't at the Megachurch.
