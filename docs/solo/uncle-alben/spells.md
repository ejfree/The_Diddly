## Spellcasting

**Tradition:** Divine
**Key Ability:** Wisdom
**Proficiency:** Expert (spell attack and spell DC), via Fourth Doctrine
**Spell DC:** 31 (10 + Wis +4 + expert bonus +17)
**Spell Attack:** +21 (Wis +4 + expert bonus +17)
**Focus Points:** 1 / 2 *(a point is spent in the export)*

**New at 13th:** the **7th rank** opens with **2 slots**, and the 6th rank gains a slot. The slot spread is now 5 cantrips and 3/3/3/3/3/**3**/**2** across ranks 1–7, plus 5 Divine Font slots. The next new spell rank (8th) arrives at 15th level.

> **⚙ ABP does nothing for his casting.** This campaign uses **Automatic Bonus Progression**, which grants item bonuses to attack rolls, weapon damage dice, AC, saves, Perception, and skills — but **nothing at all to spell DC or spell attack**. The DC 31 and +21 above are pure proficiency, and no item bonus will ever be added to them. Practically: his **weapon** side scales with both level and ABP while his **spell** side scales with level only, so as he climbs, Channel Smite / Restorative Strike / Gladiator's Roar (which keys off the higher of class or spell DC) drift toward being the better use of a turn than a save-or-suck spell. Worth keeping in mind when building future loadouts.

## Divine Font — Healing Font (additional slots)

5 additional rank-6 slots per day, usable only to prepare **Heal**. All five are filled with **Heal** in this loadout — this is where **all** of his battlefield healing capacity lives, since the standard slots above prepare none.

> **GM Note — the Font didn't move up to rank 7.** A Healing Font's extra slots are normally the highest rank the cleric can cast, which became 7th at this level. The export still lists **five rank-6 slots**. Either the Foundry entry wasn't updated, or the table is keeping the old rank. Confirm with the player.

**Focus Pool:** 2 points. Refocus with a 10-minute activity.

| Spell | Rank | Traits | Notes |

| **Athletic Rush** | 1 | cleric, focus, manipulate | Might domain. Speed bonus plus a free Athletics action — pairs with **Titan Wrestler** and his Athletics +21. |
| **Enduring Might** | 4 | cleric, focus, manipulate | Might domain. Damage-reduction reaction scaling off Strength. |

> **GM Note — orphaned domain spells.** Both of these are **Might domain** focus spells, and both are still on the sheet in the export's `Divine Focus Spells` entry. But the feats that would grant them — **Domain Initiate (Might)** and **Advanced Domain** — were replaced in an earlier export by **Warpriest's Armor** and **Cast Down** respectively (see [character.md](character.md)). Nothing on the current sheet grants domain access, yet the 2-point focus pool and both spells persist. Either the swap was meant to keep domain access (in which case a feat slot is over-spent) or these two entries are leftovers that should be deleted along with the focus pool. Resolve before Alben spends a focus point in play.

## Deity: Cayden Cailean

**Title:** The Accidental God
**Areas of Concern:** Ale, bravery, freedom, wine
**Edicts:** Drink, aid the oppressed, seek glory and adventure
**Anathema:** Waste alcohol, be mean or standoffish when drunk, oppress the vulnerable
**Religious Symbol:** Tankard
**Sacred Animal:** Hound
**Sacred Colors:** Silver, tan
**Divine Font:** Heal
**Domains:** Cities, Freedom, Indulgence, Might
**Favored Weapon:** Rapier
**Cleric Skill:** Athletics
**Sanctification:** Can be holy

Cayden Cailean is one of the Ascended — mortals who reached godhood after conquering the Test of the Starstone. In life, he was a sellsword famed for his indomitable spirit and unbridled revelry; he stumbled into the Starstone Cathedral on a dare during a night of celebration and emerged three days later, laughing, still intoxicated, and divine. He continues to enjoy a good drink, defend the vulnerable, and roam in search of adventure.
