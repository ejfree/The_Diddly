## Spellcasting

**Tradition:** Divine
**Key Ability:** Wisdom
**Proficiency:** Expert (spell attack and spell DC), via Fourth Doctrine
**Spell DC:** 30 (10 + Wis +4 + expert bonus +16)
**Spell Attack:** +20 (Wis +4 + expert bonus +16)
**Focus Points:** 2 / 2

Slot spread is unchanged at 12th — 5 cantrips and 3/3/3/3/3/2 across ranks 1–6, plus 5 Divine Font slots. The next new spell rank (7th) arrives at 13th level.

> **⚙ ABP does nothing for his casting.** This campaign uses **Automatic Bonus Progression**, which grants item bonuses to attack rolls, weapon damage dice, AC, saves, Perception, and skills — but **nothing at all to spell DC or spell attack**. The DC 30 and +20 above are pure proficiency, and no item bonus will ever be added to them. Practically: his **weapon** side scales with both level and ABP while his **spell** side scales with level only, so as he climbs, Channel Smite / Restorative Strike / Gladiator's Roar (which keys off the higher of class or spell DC) drift toward being the better use of a turn than a save-or-suck spell. Worth keeping in mind when building future loadouts.

## Deity: Cayden Cailean

**Title:** The Accidental God
**Areas of Concern:** Ale, bravery, freedom, wine
**Edicts:** Drink, aid the oppressed, seek glory and adventure
**Anathema:** Waste alcohol, be mean or standoffish when drunk, oppress the vulnerable
**Religious Symbol:** Tankard
**Sacred Animal:** Hound
**Sacred Colors:** Silver, tan
**Divine Font:** Heal
**Domains:** Cities, Freedom, Indulgence, Might
**Favored Weapon:** Rapier
**Cleric Skill:** Athletics
**Sanctification:** Can be holy

Cayden Cailean is one of the Ascended — mortals who reached godhood after conquering the Test of the Starstone. In life, he was a sellsword famed for his indomitable spirit and unbridled revelry; he stumbled into the Starstone Cathedral on a dare during a night of celebration and emerged three days later, laughing, still intoxicated, and divine. He continues to enjoy a good drink, defend the vulnerable, and roam in search of adventure.
