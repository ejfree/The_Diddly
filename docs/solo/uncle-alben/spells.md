## Deity: Cayden Cailean

**Title:** The Accidental God
**Areas of Concern:** Ale, bravery, freedom, wine
**Edicts:** Drink, aid the oppressed, seek glory and adventure
**Anathema:** Waste alcohol, be mean or standoffish when drunk, oppress the vulnerable
**Religious Symbol:** Tankard
**Sacred Animal:** Hound
**Sacred Colors:** Silver, tan
**Divine Font:** Heal
**Domains:** Cities, Freedom, Indulgence, Might
**Favored Weapon:** Rapier
**Cleric Skill:** Athletics
**Sanctification:** Can be holy

Cayden Cailean is one of the Ascended — mortals who reached godhood after conquering the Test of the Starstone. In life, he was a sellsword famed for his indomitable spirit and unbridled revelry; he stumbled into the Starstone Cathedral on a dare during a night of celebration and emerged three days later, laughing, still intoxicated, and divine. He continues to enjoy a good drink, defend the vulnerable, and roam in search of adventure.
