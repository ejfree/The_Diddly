**Note on the Dimension of Time and the Dreamlands:** Both are explicitly called out in their planar lore as resistant or immune to standard planar travel magic. The Dimension of Time is "virtually impossible to access by conventional methods of planar travel," and the Dreamlands' dreamscapes are "immune to outside entry by standard magic such as *Interplanar Teleport*." Even with a planar key in hand, Alben would need a different, more specialized method to actually reach either — the key alone wouldn't be enough. They're listed here for completeness, not as realistic destinations.

**Worth flagging narratively:** Elysium's resident divinities include Cayden Cailean himself — see below.

## How Interplanar Teleport Works (Quick Reference)

- **Spell:** *Interplanar Teleport* — rank 7, concentrate/manipulate/teleportation, uncommon, 10-minute cast, 5-foot range, up to 8 willing creatures.
- **Requirement:** A magic planar key made from material of the destination plane, used as a locus for the casting.
- **Imprecision:** The spell is highly imprecise — travelers arrive 1d20×25 miles from the last known location of one target on that plane, or at a random location if it's everyone's first visit. It does not provide return travel; casting again returns you to the previous plane (barring complications).
- **Key rarity scales with plane obscurity:** Planar keys for major, well-traveled planes (the Elemental Planes, Heaven, Hell, the Outer Rifts, etc.) are typically **uncommon** items. Keys for more obscure planes and demiplanes are often **rare**, and some may be effectively **unique**.
