# Uncle Alben — Escape Plan

> A staged bail-out procedure for when Alben is in combat and **must** disengage — outmatched, out of resources, protecting Solomon, or under orders (divine or otherwise) to survive rather than fight. This assumes things have already gone wrong: allies down or scattered, retreat the only sane option. Read top to bottom as escalating phases, not a strict script — skip a phase if the fight demands it.

---

## Phase 1 — Immediate Battlefield Disengagement

The first response to "this fight is unwinnable" — buy space and time without committing to a full retreat yet.

| Action | Source | Effect |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Dancing Shield & Shield Block** | General feat | Soak a hit (Hardness 3) while repositioning behind the shield rather than trading blows. |
| **Zealous Rush** (reaction) | Class feat | After casting any 1+ action divine spell affecting only himself/his gear, Stride up to 10 ft (or full Speed on a 2+ action spell) — a free repositioning Stride layered onto whatever he casts next. |
| **Cast a self-target buff to trigger Zealous Rush** | e.g., self-cast *Heal*, *Fly*, or *Unfettered Movement* | Combine healing/mobility with the free Stride above — this is the cheapest way to start putting distance between himself and the fight. |
| **Command** (1st-rank Combat Loadout) | Spell | Force a pursuing enemy to Stride away, Drop Prone, or otherwise waste its action — a soft control option to slow a chaser without committing more resources. |

**Goal of this phase:** Create a few rounds of breathing room. Don't burn the big-ticket escape tools yet if a Stride and a shove is enough.

---

## Phase 2 — Disguise & Concealment

If distance alone won't cut it — pursuers who know his face, a crowd he needs to vanish into, or a fight he needs to walk away from unrecognized — Alben shifts from "outrun" to "become unrecognizable."

### Current Holdings

| Item | Effect | Source |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Potion of Disguise (Lesser)** | Single action (manipulate) to drink; takes on the appearance of a specific creature type for 2d12 hours (GM rolls duration in secret). Doesn't change stats, only appearance — shrinks to minimum Small or keeps size if the target creature is Alben's size or larger. | [inventory.md](../inventory.md) |

### Recommended Additions

| Item | Level | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
| **Potion of Disguise (Moderate)** | 8 | 100 gp | Same as Lesser, but can increase size up to Large or shrink to Tiny — useful if he needs to disappear into a crowd of a very different size profile, or briefly pass as something imposing. |
| **Potion of Disguise (Greater)** | 11 | 300 gp | Lets him picture a *specific* form (including a specific individual) rather than just a creature type — the tier that actually lets him Impersonate someone particular if the escape calls for walking out as somebody else entirely, still requiring a Deception roll. |

**When to use:** After breaking line of sight (Phase 1) or immediately upon reaching a crowd/settlement — the disguise buys long-term cover, not combat invisibility, so it's best layered on top of, not instead of, an invisibility option below.

---

## Phase 3 — Invisibility

When simply not being seen is more useful than looking like someone else — the moment right before slipping away entirely.

### Current Holdings

| Item | Effect | Source |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Invisibility Potion** | Single action to drink; grants the effects of a 2nd-rank *Invisibility* spell — undetected to all creatures until Alben takes a hostile action, at which point the spell ends after that action resolves. | [inventory.md](../inventory.md) |

### Recommended Additions

| Item/Spell | Rank/Level | Notes |
|------------|-----------|-------|
| **Invisibility** (spell) | 2nd (Heightened 4th) | Adding this to the Combat Loadout would let Alben cast it on demand rather than relying solely on the one-shot potion. At Heightened 4th, the spell doesn't end on a hostile action — meaningfully better for an escape that might require one last swing of the maul on the way out. |
| **Shared Invisibility** | 3rd (Heightened 5th) | Extends invisibility to himself and up to 5 willing creatures within a 30-ft emanation — the option to bring Solomon and any surviving allies out invisible together, provided everyone stays within the emanation and no one takes a hostile action once the fight is truly over. |

---

## Phase 4 — Haste & Rapid Movement

Once invisible or disguised, closing the literal distance between "still in danger" and "actually safe" is the last mechanical hurdle before any planar option becomes relevant.

### Recommended Additions

| Item | Level | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
| **Potion of Quickness** | 8 | 90 gp | Single action to drink; grants the effects of *Haste* for 1 minute — an extra action each turn usable only for Strike, Stride, or a small set of other basic actions. Directly extends how far/fast Alben can move each round while disengaging. |
| **Quick Runner's Shirt** | 5 (Greater: 12) | 150 gp (Greater: 1,750 gp) | Worn item; Activate (2 actions, envision, 1/hour) to Stride twice with a +10-ft item bonus to Speed on those Strides (the Greater version Strides three times instead). Reusable, not consumed — a standing mobility tool rather than a one-shot potion, ideal as his default "get out of here" button once he's clear of immediate danger. |

**Recommended stacking order:** Drink the Invisibility Potion (Phase 3) → activate the Quick Runner's Shirt or drink a Potion of Quickness → Stride flat-out away from the fight. This is the fastest non-magical-teleport distance Alben can put between himself and a battlefield in a single round.

### Existing Mobility Already on the Sheet

- **Unfettered Movement** / **Fly** (4th-rank Combat Loadout, see [spells.md](../spells.md)) — already-prepared spells that serve the same purpose; use these first if the Potion of Quickness or Quick Runner's Shirt haven't been acquired yet.
- **Spiritual Transport** (5th-rank Combat Loadout, see [spells.md](../spells.md)) — 2-action divine teleportation spell, range 240 feet (1 mile when heightened to 7th, at which point line of sight to the destination is no longer required if he's been there before). This is Alben's best *currently prepared* short-range teleport: an unoccupied, visible space within range, bringing Solomon along if adjacent. Use this as the bridge between "still on the battlefield" and "somewhere safe enough to catch his breath" — it's uncommon, concentrate, manipulate, teleportation, and doesn't require a planar key since it stays on the same plane.

## Phase 5 — Interplanar Teleport to Elysium (Final Step, Future Capability)

The true last resort: leaving the plane entirely, to the one place with both stronger protection than anywhere on the Material Plane and the deepest possible thematic weight for Alben — Elysium, home plane of **Cayden Cailean** himself (see [planar.md](planar.md)).

**Current status:** Not yet possible. Two gaps must close first:

1. **Spell access** — *Interplanar Teleport* is rank 7. Alben's highest standing prepared slot is currently rank 6 (see [spells.md](../spells.md)); he needs to advance far enough to prepare a rank 7 spell at all, then actually add *Interplanar Teleport* to a loadout (likely the Combat Loadout, given its purpose here).
2. **The planar key** — Per [planar.md](planar.md), Alben owns zero planar keys, Elysium's included (Uncommon rarity). One must be acquired — ideally through a Cayden Cailean-flavored quest (a boon from the church, a relic recovered from a fallen cleric) rather than a straight purchase, consistent with the note already on file in [planar.md](planar.md).

**Once both are in place, the final escape sequence looks like this:**

1. Complete Phases 1–4 above to reach a safe, stable moment — combat fully disengaged, no immediate pursuit, ideally with Solomon adjacent and any surviving allies within reach (up to 8 willing creatures can be brought along).
2. Cast *Interplanar Teleport* (10-minute cast — this is **not** a combat action; it requires the safety already bought by Phases 1–4 to actually complete the casting uninterrupted).
3. Arrive on Elysium, imprecisely — 1d20×25 miles from the last known location of a target who's been there before, or at a random location if it's everyone's first visit.
4. Elysium itself becomes the recovery zone: the Promised Land, home to azatas, Desna, Gorum, and Cayden Cailean's own domain among them — as safe a place as exists in the whole cosmology for an orc cleric of the Accidental God to lick his wounds.

**This is deliberately the plan's capstone, not its default.** Interplanar travel is a one-way trip until cast again, imprecise by 1d20×25 miles, and burns a 10-minute casting window no mid-combat escape can actually afford — everything above exists to get Alben to the point where that casting window is even survivable.

## Quick Reference — Escalation Order

1. **Disengage** — Shield Block, Zealous Rush + self-buff, Command on a pursuer.
2. **Disguise** — Potion of Disguise (Lesser/Moderate/Greater).
3. **Vanish** — Invisibility Potion, or prepared Invisibility/Shared Invisibility.
4. **Move** — Potion of Quickness, Quick Runner's Shirt, Unfettered Movement/Fly, Spiritual Transport.
5. **Leave the plane entirely** — Interplanar Teleport to Elysium *(future capability — see Phase 5)*.
