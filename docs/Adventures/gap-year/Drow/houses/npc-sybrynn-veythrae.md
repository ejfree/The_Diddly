## Sybrynn Veythrae — Archmage of the Shadowfire Cadres

**House:** Veythrae · **Rank:** Archmage (house second) · **Level:** 14 Wizard (Evocation)
**Pronouns:** he/him · **Bodyguard:** *Ashcollar*, bound glabrezu (see below)

The highest-ranked male in Xil, and the only one whose authority does not run through a priestess. Sybrynn wrote the shadowfire bombardment protocols the entire war plan depends on — the firing tables, the darkness-saturation sequences, the timing windows that let the cadres drop area denial on a surface city without incinerating the infantry advancing under it. Every house that votes for war is voting for his math.

He is also his mother's third son and her only surviving one, which he understands to be a credential and a warning in equal measure. Male Veythrae are promoted for usefulness and retired the same way. Sybrynn has spent eleven years making himself expensive to replace, and he is privately certain that the war as currently scheduled is designed to spend him.

---

## The Position of a Male Archmage

Lolthite doctrine bars him from the priesthood, the matronage, and the Council chamber — he attends Council sessions standing behind Thalara's chair and speaks only when she turns her head. Every scrap of his power is delegated, revocable, and dependent on her continued belief that his doctrine is better than the next mage's.

This produces a very specific personality: flawlessly deferential in public, exhaustingly precise in private, and incapable of leaving a written record that could be read against him. He speaks in conditionals. He signs nothing he has not drafted three times. When he wants something to happen, he arranges for a priestess to think of it first.

**How he reads at the table:** Not a sneering villain. A brilliant, tired, careful man who has calculated his own survival odds and does not like the number.

## What He Actually Wants

1. **To survive the war he is planning.** He wants the arcane corps commanded from a rear echelon with an evacuation corridor, not from the front where Xilrath doctrine puts casters.
2. **Standing that does not route through Thalara.** Any offer of that is the lever on him.
3. **To know what the reagent is.** He builds delivery vectors — dispersal evocations, hoard-adjacency timing, breath-range calculations — for a payload the Zhaelor priesthood supplies sealed and unlabeled. He has never been permitted to assay it. He is a professional evoker being handed a black box and told the yield doesn't concern him, and it is eating him alive.
   - **GM secret:** The payload is Pale Choir material. See [insanity-plague.md](../../../setting/background/insanity-plague.md). Sybrynn is a delivery-system engineer who does not know he is a plague vector. If the PCs tell him what he has been dispersing — and prove it — he is the single most turnable senior figure in Xil, because it means Lolth's priesthood has been using him as a mule and will not need him afterward.

### Shadowfire Doctrine (special)

- **Shadowfire (proposed house mechanic).** Any fire-damage spell Sybrynn casts into or through an area of magical darkness converts to *shadowfire*: half fire, half void damage, and the darkness is not dispelled by the flame. A creature immune or resistant to fire still takes the void half at full value. Creatures with void healing are healed by the void half — Sybrynn will not use shadowfire against undead.
- **Layered Sequence (2 actions, once per encounter).** Sybrynn places a 20-foot *darkness* and immediately casts one fire spell into it as a single fluid action, gaining the shadowfire conversion and a +2 circumstance bonus to the spell DC against creatures inside.
- **Firing Tables.** With one round of preparation and line of sight, Sybrynn's area spells never include a Veythrae ally in the burst. He will happily include Xilrath infantry, goblin screens, and thralls.
- **Retreat Discipline.** At or below 100 HP he *dimension doors* or *teleports* out and lets Ashcollar cover the withdrawal. He does not fight to the death for anything. He has calculated that his doctrine survives him only if he does not.

### Tactics

Round 1: *darkness* + fireball via Layered Sequence on the largest cluster. Round 2: *haste* on Ashcollar and release it. Round 3 onward: *disintegrate* on whoever is dispelling his darkness, *mirror image* the moment anything closes, *wall of ice* to cut the room in half. He treats melee contact as a doctrinal failure and will spend a 6th-rank slot to undo it.

## Ashcollar — Bound Glabrezu Bodyguard

One of House Veythrae's six **Bound Demons** (see the auxiliaries TOE in [house-veythrae-the-shadowfire-cadres.md](house-veythrae-the-shadowfire-cadres.md)) — and the only one held on a personal rather than a house contract. It is not kept in Elvrae's pits. It stands behind Sybrynn's left shoulder.

**Appearance:** Nine feet of muscle the color of wet slate, four arms — two ending in pincers the size of a man's torso — and a dog-like head with too much intelligence in it. Around its throat is the shadowfire collar: a band of black iron that burns with dark flame, casting shadow instead of light. The collar is the contract made physical. It watches Sybrynn constantly, and drow who notice tend to assume devotion.

**Contract name:** Ashcollar. **True name:** *Vorruthax'zaal* — recorded once, sealed, and held in the Veythrae estate vault by Thalara. Not in Sybrynn's possession.

### The Contract — Nine Clauses (summary)

1. Ashcollar shall preserve the body of Sybrynn Veythrae from all harm within its power.
2. It shall take no action Sybrynn has not ordered or permitted.
3. It shall not harm Veythrae blood, nor cause Veythrae blood to be harmed by its inaction.
4. It shall not speak to any priestess of Lolth unbidden.
5. It shall receive one hour daily free of standing orders (Sybrynn's concession; it insisted; he still doesn't know why).
6. It shall not be lent, transferred, or registered to another binder.
7. Term: ninety-nine years, of which eighty-two remain.
8. Payment: one living sacrifice per lunar month, delivered from the thrall pens.
9. **The contract ends on the death of Sybrynn Veythrae, and Ashcollar is released unbound to the Abyss.**

> **The trap.** Clause 9 is the flaw, and Ashcollar found it inside the first year. It cannot kill Sybrynn. It cannot *allow* him to be killed through its own inaction. But it is not required to be *clever* on his behalf, and it is not required to volunteer anything. Eighty-two years is nothing to a glabrezu. It has been patiently, scrupulously obedient for seventeen years while cataloguing every route by which Sybrynn might die without Ashcollar having failed a clause.

**Three ambiguities it exploits:**
- *"Within its power"* — it defines its own power narrowly and honestly. It will not disclose capabilities it has not been asked about.
- *"Ordered or permitted"* — it obeys the literal words. A vague order is executed in the least helpful correct way, and it will not ask for clarification.
- *Clause 5* — one unwatched hour a day, every day, for seventeen years. It has used the time to talk. To Elvrae's pit demons through the rock. To at least one Zhaelor zealot who believes she is receiving revelation. **GM:** decide what it has arranged.

**What it wants from the PCs:** For them to kill Sybrynn — cleanly, elsewhere, and in a way Ashcollar could not have prevented. If it can speak to a PC alone (clause 5), it will bargain: information about the Council, the shadowfire protocols, the sealed vault, even the location of the true-name record. Everything it says will be true. That is what makes it dangerous.

**If Sybrynn dies:** Ashcollar is released *unbound* — not banished. It is free, hostile, and in a city it has spent seventeen years studying. Give it one round of stillness before it acts; that beat is worth more than any surprise round.

**If the collar is broken while Sybrynn lives:** The contract does not void. Ashcollar is freed from compulsion but still bound by clause 9's incentive — it wants him dead, and now it can be unhelpful without limit. It will not attack him. It will simply stop preventing things.

- Melee claw +27 [+23/+19], 3d6+13 slashing, agile
- Melee horns +25, 3d8+13 piercing
- **Innate Arcane Spells** DC 34: *dispel magic* (at will), *false vision* (at will), *confusion* ×3/day, *dimension door* ×3/day, *reverse gravity* 1/day, *veil* 1/day
- **Shadowfire Collar.** Ashcollar's melee Strikes deal an additional 2d6 shadowfire (1d6 fire, 1d6 void). The collar sheds a 20-foot aura of *darkness* it can raise or lower as a free action once per round; Ashcollar sees through this aura and so do Veythrae Shadowguard. Sybrynn's Layered Sequence can be triggered off the collar's aura.
- **Bodyguard's Interposition** (reaction, once per round). Trigger: an attack or effect would damage Sybrynn Veythrae and Ashcollar is within 15 feet. Effect: Ashcollar moves into the path and takes the damage instead. This is compulsory under clause 1 — Ashcollar has no choice, and it resents every use.
- **Obedience Gap** (special). Once per encounter, if Sybrynn gives an order that is ambiguous, vague, or shouted mid-combat, the GM may have Ashcollar execute it in the least useful technically-compliant way — moving to the wrong flank, grabbing the wrong target, sustaining the wrong effect. No clause is broken. Sybrynn cannot prove anything. Use this to telegraph the betrayal before it lands.
- **XP** 4,800

## Plot Hooks

1. **The Termination Letter.** The sealed letter in the Archmage's quarters authorizes the "termination" of a named drow mage. Pick one:
   - *(a)* It names his protégé, and Thalara is testing whether he will spend a student to keep his post.
   - *(b)* It names **Sybrynn**, is addressed to Elvrae, and he has never opened it because it is not addressed to him. The PCs can open it.
   - *(c)* It is a forgery Elvrae planted, and proving that buys Sybrynn's gratitude — the closest thing to loyalty he has to offer.
2. **The True Name.** *Vorruthax'zaal* is sealed in the estate treasury vault (Ward DC 46, *symbol of death* on entry). Whoever holds it can command Ashcollar outright. Ashcollar knows where it is and will tell the PCs freely, because it very much wants that vault opened by someone else.
3. **The Black Box.** Sybrynn will trade real intelligence — protocols, timetables, Council vote counts — for a competent assay of the Zhaelor reagent. Handing him the truth about the Pale Choir is the highest-leverage non-violent play available in D3.
4. **Council Tipping.** He cannot vote. He can make the War Bloc's timetable look like suicide to the Delay Bloc, and he will if he believes the current timetable kills him. See the 4–4 split in [council-of-houses.md](council-of-houses.md).
5. **Containment Breach.** If the party fights Veythrae in the field and drops Sybrynn below 100 HP, he leaves. Ashcollar remains, compelled to cover a withdrawal it has waited seventeen years to fail at. Let the players see it hesitate.
